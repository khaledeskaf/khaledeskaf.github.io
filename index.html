01 · PROFILE

Where academic leadership meets intelligent technology.

I am a professor, academic leader and researcher working at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, Computer Vision and higher education.

I currently serve as Professor and Dean of the College of Informatics at Midocean University, leading academic development across Artificial Intelligence, Robotics and Cybersecurity. My career spans more than three decades of teaching, research, curriculum development, quality assurance and student supervision.

My work is guided by a simple principle: emerging technology has its greatest value when it improves how people learn, decide, create and solve meaningful real-world problems.
